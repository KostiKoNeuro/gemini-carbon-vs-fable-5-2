# Credits

Pocket Kaiju is built from free assets in the Patch asset library (https://patch.gg). Every model, texture, sprite, sound and music track in the game is listed below with its author and licence. Changes made for this game: the five animated creature models had their textures resized and re-encoded (WebP) and their meshes compressed (meshopt); the unused texture maps of the two ground materials and the HDR copies of the skies were left out; models are sliced, tinted, scaled and merged at run time; sounds are pitch-shifted and layered at run time.
