Make a voxel 3D diorama of a night market in Shenzhen in the browser.

After the rain: steam over the woks, neon and lanterns, wires overhead, scooters, tiny people eating and haggling, wet asphalt reflecting the light. A tiny living world you want to pause and study.

Imagine it's an entry in a diorama contest and the jury watches it for exactly 30 seconds. As soon as the page opens: a smooth looping 30-second camera fly-through, no UI. The C key switches to a free camera and back.

Everything from cubes and in code, no models or textures. The result is one index.html, libraries from a CDN are fine.

Don't ask questions: build it, watch the whole fly-through, fix whatever looks broken.
