Make a 3D browser game "Pocket Kaiju": you are a giant monster smashing a city.

Buildings crumble into pieces, people and cars flee, the army sends tanks and helicopters, and you grow with every block you destroy. What matters most is the feeling of enormous power and juicy destruction.

Imagine this is a full game for Steam, not a demo: menus, levels, upgrades, a final boss. Surprise me.

Take models, sounds and music from the patch.gg library, not boxes made in code: it has 50,000 free assets and needs no sign-in (`npx @patchgg/cli assets search`). The result is a folder with the game and an index.html. All text in the game is in English.

Don't ask questions: build it, play it yourself, fix what's broken.
